GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you
might be wondering.

What is IdeaForge? +

IdeaForge is a creative workspace that helps content creators discover ideas, write stronger hooks, develop scripts, and organize future content.

Who can use IdeaForge? +

Anyone creating content: YouTubers, TikTok and Instagram creators, bloggers, small businesses, educators, and personal brands.

Do I need to be an experienced creator? +

Not at all. IdeaForge is designed to be approachable whether you're publishing your first post or planning an established content series.

Is there a free plan? +

Yes. IdeaForge currently offers access to its core creative tools during beta, including idea generation, hook creation, script assistance, and your personal content library. Additional plans are in development.

Does IdeaForge publish content for me? +

IdeaForge helps you plan and create. You stay in control of reviewing, editing, and publishing your content.
